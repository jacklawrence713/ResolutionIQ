Privacy Policy

Loss Mitigation Rules Engine
Effective Date: March 1, 2026 · Version 1.0
Plain-Language Summary: ResolutionIQ processes borrower loan data solely to generate loss mitigation eligibility evaluations for the Servicer. We do not sell data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it to train AI models. Servicers own their Loan Data. Authorized Users' account data is used only to operate and secure the platform.

1Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how ResolutionIQ ("we," "us," "Provider") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the ResolutionIQ Loss Mitigation Rules Engine ("Engine," "Service"). This Policy applies to: (a) Servicer organizations that subscribe to or license the Engine; and (b) Authorized Users (employees and contractors) who access the Engine on behalf of a Servicer.

ResolutionIQ operates as a service provider / data processor with respect to Loan Data — the Servicer is the data controller or business responsible for that data.

2Information We Collect

2.1 Authorized User Account Data

  • Name, email address, employer/organization name
  • Authentication credentials (passwords stored as salted cryptographic hashes; we never store plaintext passwords)
  • OAuth tokens (Google, Microsoft) for SSO users
  • Role and permission assignments within the platform
  • Login timestamps and session activity for security logging

2.2 Loan Data (Servicer-Controlled)

  • Mortgage loan fields entered by Authorized Users: loan number, borrower name, UPB, interest rate, payment amounts, income, hardship information, property details, investor designation, and related data
  • Evaluation outputs: eligibility results, audit trails, approval terms, regulatory citations
  • Case notes, assignment records, version history, and SLA data created in the platform

2.3 Usage & Technical Data

  • Log data: IP addresses, browser type, pages accessed, features used, errors encountered
  • Aggregate, anonymized usage metrics (e.g., how many evaluations per investor type, feature adoption rates)

4Data Sharing & Sub-Processors

We engage the following categories of sub-processors to operate the Service:

  • Cloud infrastructure & database hosting (e.g., Supabase / AWS) — for data storage and application hosting
  • Authentication providers (e.g., Google OAuth, Microsoft OAuth) — solely for user authentication
  • Email delivery — for transactional notifications (case assignments, SLA alerts)
  • Error monitoring — for platform reliability and debugging (anonymized stack traces only)

All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements and are required to maintain appropriate security standards. We do not share Loan Data with any third party beyond what is necessary to operate the Service.

5Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Compliance

Loan Data processed through the Engine may constitute non-public personal information ("NPI") under the GLBA. ResolutionIQ operates as a service provider to Servicers under 15 U.S.C. §6802(b)(2) and is contractually prohibited from using or disclosing NPI other than to carry out the purposes for which Servicer disclosed it. Servicers retain responsibility for their own GLBA compliance obligations, including providing required privacy notices to borrowers.

6Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Row-level security policies ensuring Servicers can only access their own data
  • Multi-factor authentication support for Authorized Users
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability monitoring
  • Incident response procedures with notification to affected Servicers within 72 hours of confirmed breach

7Data Retention

Loan Data and evaluation records are retained for the duration of the Servicer's active subscription plus thirty (30) days following termination to allow data export. Upon written request following termination, we will delete or return Loan Data within sixty (60) days. Account Data is retained for the period necessary to manage the account and as required by applicable law. Usage logs are retained for a maximum of twelve (12) months.

8Servicer and User Rights

Servicers may access, export, and delete their Loan Data through the platform at any time during the active subscription. Authorized Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their account data by contacting privacy@resolutioniq.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within thirty (30) calendar days.

9Cookies & Tracking

The Engine uses session cookies necessary for authentication and maintaining logged-in state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or behavioral analytics cookies. Users may disable cookies in their browser settings but doing so will prevent login to the platform.

10Children's Privacy

The Engine is a business-to-business service intended for use by licensed mortgage servicers. It is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to active Servicers at least 30 days in advance via email or in-platform notice. The current version is always accessible within the platform settings.
