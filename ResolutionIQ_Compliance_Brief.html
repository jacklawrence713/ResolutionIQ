It delivers node-by-node audit reasoning, full terms calculation, document checklists, and AI-assisted underwriting in a single browser-based tool — fully current to the latest guidelines as of Q1 2026. Following a comprehensive regulatory compliance review conducted in March 2026, the engine achieved an overall confidence rating of ~98.5% across all investors, with individual investor scores ranging from 98–99%. No comparable free-access multi-investor tool exists in the market.

Compliance Improvements — March 2026 Review

The following changes were implemented during the March 2026 compliance review, verified against primary regulatory sources including Bulletin 2026-2, ML 2025-12, USDA Final Rule (Apr 2025), and VA Circular 26-25-2.

FHLMC
Disaster Flex Mod dual DLQ gate (§9206.1(c)(v) — Bulletin 2026-2, eff. Feb 11, 2026)
Added ≥90 days DLQ at evaluation requirement. Previously the engine only checked <60 DLQ at time of disaster. This was a live compliance gap for all evaluations performed after February 11, 2026.
FHLMC
Forbearance increment reduction (§9203.3 — Bulletin 2026-2, eff. May 1, 2026)
Terms output updated from "1–6 months per increment" to "1–3 months per increment." Cumulative 12-month cap unchanged.
FHLMC
Streamlined Flex Mod solicitation window gate (§9206.7)
Added servicer-side solicitation letter requirement (90–105 DLQ window) as an eligibility node with corresponding UI toggle.
FHLMC
Failed TPP definition clarified (§9206.5)
Node label and UI updated to specify "servicer-cancelled (non-performing)" TPP — borrower-initiated withdrawals confirmed not to trigger the 12-month ineligibility.
FHA
Payment Supplement 36-month cooldown (ML 2024-02)
Added new field and gate: prior Payment Supplement must be ≥36 months ago. Previously only the general 24-month home retention cooldown was applied — incorrect for PS re-eligibility.
FHA
Payment Supplement 30% PC cap (ML 2024-02)
Payment Supplement uses a Partial Claim — comboCapPass (30% statutory cap) now applied to PS eligibility. Previously missing.
FHA
DLQ upper cap for home retention options (§III.A.2.k)
Added DLQ ≤12 months maximum gate to all HAMP home retention options. Previously only the lower bound (≥4 months) was enforced.
FHA
RPP 150% PITI cap (§III.A.2.f)
Repayment Plan now auto-computes and gates on total payment ≤150% of current PITI.
FNMA
Standard Flex Mod BRP gate and mod seasoning (D2-3.2-06)
BRP documentation required for <90 DLQ path. 12-month mod seasoning blocks Standard Flex Mod if recently modified.
FNMA
Payment Deferral cumulative cap fix (D2-3.2-04)
Changed from cumulativeDeferred <12 to (cumulativeDeferred + dlq) ≤12, correctly accounting for the current event in the lifetime cap.
FNMA
MTMLTV informational audit node (LL-2024-02)
Post-capitalization MTMLTV now appears in the Standard Flex Mod eligibility audit trail showing ≥50% (rate reduction) and >50% (forbearance) thresholds.
VA
Last-mod seasoning split (§36.4315(a)(3))
First modification: ≥12 months from loan origination. Subsequent modifications: ≥12 months from last mod date. vaLastModDate field added for auto-computation.
VA
Disaster Extend Modification fix (Circular 26-24-3 §3.a)
Removed unverified dlqGe12ContractualPayments gate. Confirmed 12-month maturity extension cap. Clarified this does not require prior Disaster Modification as prerequisite.
VA
Compromise Sale VA RLC approval node
Added explicit audit node: servicers have NO delegated authority — all Compromise Sales require VA Regional Loan Center approval (Circular 26-24-2).
VA
VASP discontinuation (Circular 26-25-2)
VASP marked DISCONTINUED May 1, 2025. Pipeline transition rules (Apr 30 cutoff, Aug 31 TPP deadline, Sep 30 payment deadline) documented. VA Home Loan Program Reform Act partial claim authority noted as PENDING.
USDA
Final Rule compliance (7 CFR §3555.304, eff. Apr 14, 2025)
Removed 12-payment loan age requirement (no regulatory basis). Added 55% total DTI cap. MRA cap basis changed to UPB at initial default (not origination UPB). 36-month post-mod performance requirement surfaced in terms output.

ResolutionIQ consolidates this into one interface, one document checklist format, and one regulatory citation standard — without replacing investor submission systems.

Competitive Moat

Speed of regulatory updates Bulletin 2026-2 was issued February 11, 2026. The new dual DLQ requirement for FHLMC Disaster Flex Mod (§9206.1(c)(v)) was incorporated into ResolutionIQ within days. Enterprise platforms typically require a vendor change request, development sprint, QA cycle, and release — a process that takes 60–120 days. During that window, servicers using enterprise platforms were making incorrect eligibility determinations for disaster-impacted borrowers.
