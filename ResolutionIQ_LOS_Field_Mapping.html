Integration Notes

  • PMMS Rate: Not available in any LOS — must be manually updated weekly or configured as a servicer-wide default in ResolutionIQ Settings. Freddie Mac publishes the PMMS every Thursday at freddiemac.com/pmms.
  • JSON import: ResolutionIQ accepts camelCase, snake_case, and common business name variants for all fields. The engine performs auto-mapping on import — you do not need exact field name matches.
  • Null handling: Missing optional fields default to 0 or false. Missing required fields will lower the data completeness score and may cause some options to not be evaluated.
  • Date format: ISO 8601 preferred (YYYY-MM-DD). Most US date formats (MM/DD/YYYY) are also accepted.
  • Rate format: Interest rates should be entered as decimal percentages (6.75 not 0.0675).
