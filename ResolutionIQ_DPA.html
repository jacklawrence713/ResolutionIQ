Data Processing Agreement

GLBA · CCPA · State Privacy Law Compliance
Version 1.0 · Effective Upon Execution
Purpose: This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Subscription License Agreement or Source Code Purchase Agreement and governs ResolutionIQ's processing of non-public personal information ("NPI") and other personal data on behalf of the Servicer. This DPA is required by most servicer vendor management programs prior to production use.

This DPA is entered into between ResolutionIQ ("Processor") and the Servicer identified in the applicable agreement ("Controller / Business"), effective as of the date of the applicable agreement.

1Definitions

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including borrower NPI as defined under the GLBA.

"Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, retrieval, disclosure, and deletion.

"Servicer Personal Data" means Personal Data that the Servicer submits to the Engine for processing, including borrower name, loan data, and financial information.

"Security Incident" means any accidental or unlawful access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure of Servicer Personal Data.

"Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Servicer Personal Data.

2Processing Instructions

Processor shall process Servicer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Servicer and solely for the purpose of providing the Engine's loss mitigation eligibility evaluation functionality. Processor shall not process Servicer Personal Data for any other purpose, including for Processor's own commercial purposes, without the Servicer's prior written consent.

If Processor is required by applicable law to process Servicer Personal Data in a manner inconsistent with Servicer's instructions, Processor shall notify Servicer before such processing (unless prohibited by law from doing so).

4Confidentiality of Processing

Processor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to process Servicer Personal Data are subject to confidentiality obligations. Processor shall implement appropriate vetting of personnel with access to Servicer Personal Data. Access is limited to personnel who require it to provide the Service.

5Security Measures

Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:

  1. Encryption: TLS 1.2+ for data in transit; AES-256 for data at rest;
  2. Access Controls: Role-based access, row-level security, principle of least privilege;
  3. Authentication: Support for multi-factor authentication; secure credential storage;
  4. Network Security: Firewall controls, DDoS protection, intrusion detection;
  5. Vulnerability Management: Regular security assessments, dependency scanning;
  6. Backup & Recovery: Regular backups with tested recovery procedures.

6Sub-processors

Servicer provides general authorization for Processor to engage sub-processors for operational purposes. Processor's current sub-processors are listed in Exhibit A. Processor shall notify Servicer at least 30 days before engaging a new sub-processor that will process Servicer Personal Data. Servicer may object to a new sub-processor within 15 days of notification; if the parties cannot resolve the objection, Servicer may terminate the Agreement. Processor shall ensure all sub-processors are bound by DPA obligations at least as protective as those in this DPA.

7Security Incident Notification

Upon becoming aware of a confirmed Security Incident, Processor shall notify Servicer without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours. Notification shall include: (a) nature and scope of the incident; (b) categories and approximate number of individuals affected; (c) measures taken or proposed to address the incident. Processor shall cooperate with Servicer's reasonable requests in connection with the incident and any required regulatory notifications.

8Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Processor shall assist Servicer in responding to requests from individuals exercising rights under applicable privacy laws (access, correction, deletion, portability). Servicer acknowledges that the primary relationship is between the Servicer and borrowers, and that Processor's role is limited to the technical capability to export or delete data upon Servicer's instruction.

9Data Retention & Deletion

Upon termination or expiration of the applicable agreement, Processor shall make Servicer Personal Data available for export for thirty (30) days. Upon Servicer's written request, Processor shall delete or return all Servicer Personal Data within sixty (60) days and certify such deletion in writing. Processor may retain Servicer Personal Data as required by applicable law, provided such data is segregated and its use restricted to the legal obligation only.

10Audits & Compliance

Servicer may, upon reasonable notice (no less than 30 days) and at Servicer's expense, audit Processor's compliance with this DPA no more than once per calendar year. Processor shall provide access to relevant documentation, systems, and personnel. In lieu of an on-site audit, Processor may provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent certification as evidence of compliance.

11GLBA Specific Obligations

To the extent Servicer Personal Data constitutes NPI under the GLBA: (a) Processor shall not disclose NPI to any third party except as permitted under 15 U.S.C. §6802(b)(2) for the purpose of carrying out the service for which NPI was disclosed; (b) Processor acknowledges it is prohibited from using NPI for independent marketing purposes; (c) Processor shall maintain appropriate safeguards consistent with the GLBA Safeguards Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 314).

12Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the same law as the applicable agreement. Nothing in this DPA reduces any rights or remedies available to data subjects under applicable privacy law.
