Common Objections

"How do we know the guidelines are current?"
Every result shows the specific regulatory citation with the version applied. Subscription includes guaranteed 30-day updates on material guideline changes. The Regulatory Index document lists every citation in the engine by section.
"Our examiners won't accept software output."
The engine documents your specialist's reasoning — it doesn't replace it. The Audit tab generates the criterion-by-criterion analysis your examiners need, all traceable to the specific guideline section. It's better documentation than a manually-written memo.
"We already have a spreadsheet system."
Spreadsheets don't update automatically when ML 2025-06 changes the P&I target, or when VA rescinds the waterfall, or when Freddie Mac issues Bulletin 2026-2. One compliance miss costs more than the annual subscription.
"We need LOS integration — we can't have a separate system."
We have a field mapping guide for Encompass and BytePro — most fields import in minutes. Full LOS integration is available as an add-on service. Alternatively, the source code purchase gives your team the codebase to build native integration.
"What about data security? We can't put borrower data in a third-party system."
We have a Data Processing Agreement ready to execute. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Row-level security isolates your data from all other servicers. For maximum control, the outright purchase option lets you host entirely on your own infrastructure.
"What happens if you shut down or change pricing?"
The source code purchase eliminates vendor dependency entirely — you own the engine, you host it, you maintain it. For subscribers, annual pricing is locked for the contract term. We can include an escrow arrangement for Commercial Rights purchasers.
