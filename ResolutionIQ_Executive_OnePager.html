What It Does

ResolutionIQ is an automated loss mitigation eligibility engine that evaluates any delinquent mortgage loan against the complete regulatory requirements of all five major investors — instantly. Given a loan's financial profile and hardship details, it produces a ranked waterfall of eligible workout options with full audit-node justification for every decision, exactly as required by investor guidelines.

Regulatory Accuracy
Rules built from primary sources: HUD 4000.1, USDA HB-1-3555, VA M26-4, Fannie Mae Servicing Guide (LL-2024-02), Freddie Mac Guide Ch. 9200 (Bulletin 2026-2, Feb 2026). 16 compliance fixes applied through current review cycle. 97–99% accuracy validated on standard production cases.
Complete Coverage
Every option for every investor: modifications (30yr, 40yr, combo, disaster, flex), deferrals, forbearance, repayment plans, partial claims, payment supplements, short sale, deed-in-lieu, and more. Waterfall priority ordering per investor hierarchy — all five investors in a single evaluation pass.
Audit-Ready Output
Every eligibility decision accompanied by a full audit trail of pass/fail nodes with regulatory citations. Designed for servicer examination readiness and investor repurchase defense. No black-box decisions — every conclusion is traceable to its governing guideline.
